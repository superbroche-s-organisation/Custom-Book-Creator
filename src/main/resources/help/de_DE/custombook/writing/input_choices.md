# Predefined choices

Enter one complete choice per line, for example:

- `I accept the terms.`
- `I decline the terms.`

Enter the sentences directly in the field, without bullet markers. Empty lines are ignored; duplicate choices are removed. At most 16 distinct choices are kept. Each choice is plain text and is limited by Maximum characters for this zone (at most 1,024).

PRESET lets players select a choice without typing custom text. BOTH offers the choices and allows players to type or edit text. FREE does not display the choice list.

A choice replaces this zone's value; it does not append text, edit other zones or automatically sign the book. A PRESET default must match a choice exactly. Writing permission, signatures and the book-wide 4,096-character limit still apply.
